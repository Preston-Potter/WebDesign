Info

Snowbowl is a ski resort located just outside of Missoula Montana. It is open in the winter for skiing/snowboarding, and also in the summer for hiking and mountain biking. It has four chairlifts, and the peak reaches about 2,600 vertical feet. They are known for their special events like the Gelende jump, and their pizza.
