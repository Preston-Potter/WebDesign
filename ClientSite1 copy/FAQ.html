FAQs

How do I register to vote?

Vote.org has a quick register tool that makes it easy to sign up in a couple of minutes!

I am in college out of state, how should I vote?

According to Vote.org, students should vote in whatever location they consider their "residence", which can be either your hometown or where you go to school

I am 18 and still in high school, how can I learn more about voting at my school?

This artice from Edsource.org explains some legislature that was passed in order for schools to provide education on voting.
